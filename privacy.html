Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your account, your events and your galleries, and nothing more.
  • Photos and videos stay private. Galleries are reached only by their private links, never listed or searchable.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Galleries are deleted automatically when their plan period ends, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

This Privacy Policy explains how CK Enterprise Group ("IDOIR", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the IDOIR Photobooth app for iPhone and iPad, our online event galleries and our website (together, the "Service").

The Service has two kinds of users. Hosts are the photobooth businesses and individuals who create an account, set up events and run the booth. Guests are the people who use the booth at an event, receive their photos or open an event gallery. Guests don't need an account.

1. Information we collect

From hosts

  • Account information: your name, email address, password (stored only in encrypted form), and studio or business name.
  • Event information: event names, dates, notes, booth settings, print designs and any images, videos or PNG frames you upload.
  • Subscription information: your plan, purchase dates and renewal status. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your card details.

Captured at events

  • Photos, GIFs, boomerangs and videos taken in the booth, including audio recorded with video messages.
  • Stickers and print layouts guests add to their captures.
  • Contact details a guest types in to receive their photos by text or email. These are passed to your device's Messages or Mail app to send, and we don't keep them.

Collected automatically

  • Usage and diagnostic information such as capture counts per event (used to apply plan limits), app version, device type and crash reports.
  • Gallery access such as the time a gallery link was opened, used to keep galleries secure and working.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect precise location. Prints and gallery images are created as new image files without GPS or camera location data.
  • We don't use facial recognition to identify anyone. The app detects where faces are in a photo, on the device, only to crop it so heads aren't cut off; no face data is stored or sent to us.
  • We don't access your photo library, contacts or other apps' data.

2. Camera and microphone

The app asks for permission to use the camera to take photos and videos, and the microphone to record video messages. You can turn either permission off in iOS Settings at any time; booth modes that need it will stop working until you turn it back on.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: run the booth, create prints, send captures, and host event galleries.
  • To manage accounts and subscriptions, including plan limits and gallery periods.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To contact hosts about their account, purchases or important changes. We'll only send marketing emails if you opt in, and you can opt out at any time.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't use photos, videos or audio to train artificial intelligence models, and we don't use them for advertising.

4. How information is shared

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • With service providers who process it on our behalf under contract:

    SupabaseAccount sign-in, database and storage for events and gallery files
    RevenueCatManages subscription status

  • Within an event, as the host sets it up: guests can see captures in that event's gallery, either everyone's or only their own, and anyone with a gallery link (and passcode, if set) can view it.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or IDOIR.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale, where the buyer agrees to honor this policy.

5. Hosts and guests

For photos and videos taken at an event, the host decides how the booth and gallery are used, and we process those captures on the host's behalf. Hosts are responsible for letting guests know a photobooth is in use and that captures may appear in an event gallery. Guests who want a capture removed can ask the host, or contact us at info@ckbusinessagency.com and we'll pass the request on or act on it ourselves.

6. How long we keep information

  • Event galleries stay online for the period included in the host's plan (3 months on Basic, 6 months on Pro, 12 months on Business, counted from the event date) and are then deleted from our servers, normally within 7 days.
  • Captures on the host's device stay there until the host deletes them or the app.
  • Account information is kept while the account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your account, events and galleries within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as purchase records).
  • Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, normally within 90 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app under Account › Delete Account. This removes your account, events and galleries.
  • Delete captures or galleries at any time from the event in the app.
  • Access, correct or get a copy of your information by emailing info@ckbusinessagency.com.
  • Cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, including those under the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar U.S. state laws, or the GDPR and UK GDPR: to know, access, correct, delete or port your information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running a secure service, your consent (for camera, microphone and marketing), and legal obligations. You can contact us at info@ckbusinessagency.com to exercise any right, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

8. Children

The Service is for adults who host events and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children to create accounts. Children may appear in event photos taken by their families and other guests; hosts should make sure a parent or guardian is comfortable with this. If you believe a child's information should be removed, contact info@ckbusinessagency.com and we'll delete it.

9. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit, access controls on our database and storage, private and unlisted gallery links, optional gallery passcodes, and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep gallery links private.

10. International transfers

We're based in the United States and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, for transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. We'll post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and tell hosts in the app or by email before significant changes take effect.
